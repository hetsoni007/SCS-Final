## The short version

All three are RBI-authorised, all three cover UPI and cards, and all three will work for a typical Indian WooCommerce store. The differences that actually matter in 2026: **Razorpay** has the smoothest WooCommerce plugin experience and strong UPI success rates; **Cashfree** undercuts on standard pricing (1.95%) and leads on payouts/settlements; **PayU** is strongest for international cards and EMI/BNPL — but its WordPress.org plugin was withdrawn in mid-2026 and its ₹4,999/year AMC adds a fixed cost the others don't charge.

## What actually matters for a WooCommerce store

**Payment success rate beats fee differences.** The gap between 1.95% and 2% on ₹3 lakh of monthly sales is ₹150. A 2% difference in UPI success rate on the same volume is ₹6,000 of lost orders. Ask any gateway for their current success-rate numbers for your category before you pick on price.

**Plugin quality is your integration cost.** Razorpay and Cashfree both maintain official WooCommerce plugins, which makes setup a configuration task rather than a development project. With PayU's WordPress plugin currently unavailable, integrating it on WooCommerce means custom work — fine if you have a strong reason (international mix, specific EMI needs), otherwise friction you don't need.

**Settlement speed is cash flow.** If your margins are thin and inventory turns fast, Cashfree's faster settlement options are worth real money. If you're comfortable on a 1–2 day cycle, it's a non-issue.

**GST on gateway fees.** All providers add 18% GST on their fees — the calculator above shows base fees, so add GST when budgeting margins.

## Multiple gateways: when it's worth the complexity

Some stores run two gateways — one primary, one fallback — on the logic that gateway downtime shouldn't mean store downtime. For high-volume stores that's sound engineering. For a typical small store, it doubles your reconciliation work, splits your settlement reporting, and adds a second integration to maintain, for an outage that might cost you an afternoon a year. Start with one well-chosen gateway; add a second only when the revenue at risk justifies the operational overhead.

## International sales from an Indian store

If a meaningful share of your customers pay with non-Indian cards, the calculus shifts. International transaction rates run higher (typically 3%+ across providers), settlement adds currency conversion, and RBI compliance around export receivables applies. PayU's strength here is real, but Razorpay and Cashfree both handle international cards too — the deciding factor is usually approval rates for your specific corridor, which you can only learn by asking each provider with your actual numbers.

## Setting it up right (the part people skip)

-   **Webhook verification:** your store must verify payment webhooks server-side — trusting the redirect alone is how stores ship goods for failed payments.
-   **Refund flows:** test them before launch, not during your first dispute.
-   **GST-compliant invoicing:** wire your invoice numbering and tax fields to the order data from day one.
-   **Failure UX:** a clear retry path on failed payments recovers a meaningful share of drop-offs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which payment gateway is cheapest for WooCommerce in India?

On standard published rates, Cashfree (1.95%) edges out Razorpay (~2%), while PayU adds a ₹4,999/year AMC on top of transaction fees. At typical small-store volumes the rate difference is small — success rates and settlement speed usually matter more.

### Does Razorpay work with WooCommerce?

Yes — Razorpay maintains an official WooCommerce plugin, making integration a configuration task. It's the smoothest setup of the three for most stores.

### Can I use PayU with WooCommerce in 2026?

PayU's WordPress.org plugin was withdrawn in June 2026, so there's currently no supported official plugin — integrating it means custom development. Choose it only if you specifically need its international/EMI strengths.

### Do these gateways support UPI AutoPay for subscriptions?

Razorpay and Cashfree both support UPI AutoPay for recurring payments — relevant if your store sells subscriptions or memberships.
