## Consulting is not the same as development

A development engagement delivers a product. A consulting engagement delivers **a decision, a plan or an unblocked team**. The output is often a document, a review or a short burst of hands-on work alongside your own engineers.

That difference matters when you buy. You are paying for judgement, so seniority counts far more than headcount.

## What React Native consulting usually covers

-   **Architecture review.** Is the app structured to scale, or is it accumulating debt? Navigation, state, data layer, native modules.
-   **Performance audits.** Slow start-up, janky lists, memory growth and oversized bundles are common and usually fixable.
-   **New Architecture migration.** Planning the move, checking library compatibility and sequencing the work. Our [New Architecture guide](/blog/react-native-new-architecture-2026) explains what is involved.
-   **Release and CI/CD setup.** Automated builds, signing, store submission and over-the-air updates. See [CI/CD for React Native](/blog/ci-cd-react-native).
-   **Build or rebuild decisions.** An honest view on whether to fix the current codebase or start again.
-   **Technology choices.** React Native against Flutter, Kotlin Multiplatform or native for your case. We compare them in [React Native vs Flutter](/blog/react-native-vs-flutter-2026) and [Kotlin Multiplatform vs React Native](/blog/kotlin-multiplatform-vs-react-native).
-   **Team support.** Code review, pairing and hiring help for an in-house team that is new to mobile.

## When a consultant is the right call

Hire a consultant when:

1.  **You already have a team** and need senior input, not more hands.
2.  **Something specific is stuck**: a release, a performance problem, a migration.
3.  **You face a one-off decision** with long consequences, such as a rebuild or a platform choice.
4.  **You are about to hire or outsource** and want an independent view of scope first.

Hire a development team instead when you need a product built end to end and have no engineers to carry it. Our guide to [hiring React Native developers](/blog/hire-react-native-developers) covers that route.

## What a good engagement looks like

-   **A written question.** "Why is our Android start-up slow?" is a brief. "Help with our app" is not.
-   **Access to the code.** A consultant who has not read the repository is guessing.
-   **A fixed window.** Days or weeks with a defined end, not an open retainer by default.
-   **A concrete deliverable.** A prioritised list of findings with effort estimates, not a slide deck of principles.
-   **A handover.** Your team should be able to continue without the consultant.

## Questions to ask before you sign

-   Have you shipped React Native apps to both stores, and can I see them?
-   Who exactly will do the work, and how senior are they?
-   What will I have in my hands at the end?
-   How do you handle findings that suggest we should not continue with you?
-   Can you work inside our tools and review process?

## How pricing works

Consulting is normally priced by time, because the scope is a question and not a feature list. A build is better priced as a fixed scope. If a review leads to build work, the estimate should follow the same logic as any other project: weeks of engineering multiplied by a rate. ## Frequently asked questions

### What does a React Native consultant do?

They review architecture and performance, plan migrations, set up release pipelines, advise on technology choices and support in-house teams. The output is a decision, a plan or an unblocked team more than a finished product.

### When should I hire a React Native consultant instead of an agency?

When you already have engineers and need senior judgement on a specific problem or decision. If you need a product built from scratch with no team of your own, a development team is the better fit.

### How long does a consulting engagement last?

Typically days to a few weeks, with a defined question and a defined end. Longer support can follow, but it should be a choice and not the default.

### Can a consultant work with my existing developers?

Yes. Code review, pairing and working inside your existing tools and process are a normal part of consulting, and the aim is to leave your team able to continue alone.

### Do I need a consultant before rebuilding my app?

An independent review before a rebuild is usually worth it. Rebuilds are expensive, and many problems that prompt them can be fixed in the existing codebase.
