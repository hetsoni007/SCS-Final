## How to read any India price

Every honest estimate is **weeks of engineering multiplied by a rate**. When two Indian vendors quote very different numbers, ask each for the weeks. A low quote usually assumes fewer weeks, a more junior team, or a scope that leaves out testing, the admin side or compliance.

The figures below use the method behind our [app cost calculator](/app-cost-calculator/), with an indicative blended rate of $360 to $600 per week for a senior team. They are planning ranges in US dollars, not quotes. Convert at the current rate for a rupee figure, and note that GST applies to software services billed in India.

## India-specific factors that change the cost

### Payments

Most Indian apps need UPI alongside cards, net banking and wallets, usually through a payment gateway. Refunds, failed transactions and reconciliation take real work. Our [payment gateway guide](/blog/woocommerce-payment-gateway-india) covers the choices.

### Data protection

The DPDP Act brings consent, notice and data-handling duties for apps with Indian users. Build them in from the start. See our [DPDP readiness guide](/blog/dpdp-act-app-compliance-india).

### Fintech rules

Lending, wallets and payment products carry RBI requirements that affect architecture, hosting and partners. See [RBI compliance for fintech apps](/blog/rbi-fintech-app-compliance-india).

### Device and network range

Indian users span low-cost Android phones and patchy networks as well as flagship devices. Testing on lower-end devices, smaller app size and an offline-tolerant design add effort and are usually worth it.

### Languages

Supporting Hindi and regional languages affects design, content and testing. Plan for it early, because retrofitting is slower.

## Why the cheapest quote often costs more

-   **Rebuilds.** An app that cannot scale or pass store review gets built twice.
-   **Missing scope.** Admin tools, testing and release work appear later as change requests.
-   **Seniority.** A senior team costs more per week and needs fewer weeks.

Compare vendors against the same written scope. Our guide to [scoping an app](/blog/how-to-scope-an-app) shows how to write one.

## How to bring the cost down

-   Build a true MVP around one core loop.
-   Use one shared codebase for iOS and Android.
-   Start on a managed backend.
-   Buy commodity pieces such as auth, payments and push.
-   Phase compliance-heavy features only if the rules allow it.

## Costs after launch

Budget roughly 15 to 20 percent of the build cost per year for maintenance, plus gateway fees, hosting, SMS and store fees. See [app maintenance cost](/blog/app-maintenance-cost).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to build an app in India in 2026?

It depends on scope. Using our method, a lean MVP is about 7 weeks and roughly $2.5k to $4k, and a market-ready product with payments, an admin dashboard and a custom backend is about 25 weeks and roughly $9k to $15k. These are indicative ranges for a senior team.

### Why do app development quotes in India vary so much?

Vendors assume different scope, team seniority and rates. Ask each for the weeks behind the number and compare against the same written feature list.

### Does GST apply to app development in India?

GST applies to software services billed in India. Confirm the current rate and treatment for your case with your accountant.

### What India-specific features add cost?

UPI and gateway payments, DPDP consent and data handling, RBI requirements for fintech, support for lower-end Android devices and poor networks, and regional languages.
