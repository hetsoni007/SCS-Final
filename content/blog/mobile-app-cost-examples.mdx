## How these examples are calculated

All three examples use the same method as our [app cost calculator](/app-cost-calculator/): estimate the weeks of senior engineering, then apply an indicative blended rate of **$1,800 to $3,200 per week**. If you want the full logic first, read [how an app cost estimator works](/blog/app-cost-estimator).

These are **planning ranges, not quotes**. A fixed price is confirmed after a short discovery call once your requirements are clear.

## Example 1: A lean MVP

**The product:** a simple booking or community app for one audience, built to test demand.

-   Stage: MVP
-   Features: user accounts and push notifications
-   Backend: managed (no custom API)
-   Platforms: cross-platform, standard design

**Result: about 7 weeks, roughly $13k to $22k.**

Add payments and the same app becomes about 9 weeks and roughly $16k to $29k. That is the typical pattern: **one feature that touches money costs more than several that do not**.

**Where to save:** keep the backend managed, use a standard design system and ship the one loop that proves people want it.

## Example 2: A market-ready product

**The product:** a funded product with customers paying, such as a retail-ops tool or a service marketplace.

-   Stage: market-ready
-   Features: accounts, payments, push notifications, an admin dashboard and analytics
-   Backend: custom API
-   Platforms: cross-platform, custom UI

**Result: about 25 weeks, roughly $45k to $80k.**

Add chat and maps in place of analytics and the total moves to about 28 weeks and roughly $50k to $90k. Real-time features and location add integration and testing work that is easy to underestimate.

**Where to save:** buy commodity pieces (auth, payments, push) instead of building them, and phase the admin dashboard so version one needs only what operations truly use.

## Example 3: An AI-powered platform

**The product:** a multi-feature platform with AI at its core, such as an assistant, a recommendation engine or an automated workflow.

-   Stage: scale
-   Features: accounts, payments, chat, push notifications, admin dashboard, AI integration and analytics
-   Backend: real-time at scale
-   Platforms: cross-platform, premium animated design

**Result: about 48 weeks, roughly $86k to $154k.**

At this tier, cost tracks the **integration and reliability surface** more than the screen count. Model usage, latency, monitoring and compliance each add work. Our [AI app development cost guide](/blog/ai-app-development-cost-2026) goes deeper on that.

**Where to save:** prove the AI feature on a narrow use case first. Many teams discover that one well-scoped AI feature delivers most of the value of a platform-wide build.

## The pattern across all three

| | MVP | Market-ready | AI platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks | about 7 | about 25 | about 48 |
| Indicative range | $13k to $22k | $45k to $80k | $86k to $154k |
| Biggest cost driver | payments | backend and real-time | AI and scale |
| Best first saving | managed backend | buy, do not build | narrow the AI scope |

## Costs the examples do not include

-   **Maintenance:** about 15 to 20 percent of build cost per year ([details here](/blog/app-maintenance-cost)).
-   **Third-party usage:** payments, SMS, maps and AI calls scale with users.
-   **Launch and marketing:** budget for it separately.
-   **Store submission:** use our [launch checklist](/blog/app-store-launch-checklist) to avoid rejections that cost weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to build a mobile app in 2026?

It depends on scope. Using our method, a lean MVP is about 7 weeks and roughly $13k to $22k, a market-ready product is about 25 weeks and roughly $45k to $80k, and an AI-powered platform is about 48 weeks and roughly $86k to $154k. These are indicative ranges, confirmed by a discovery call.

### What makes one app cost more than another?

Stage, the number of expensive features (payments, chat, maps, AI, offline), backend complexity, the number of platforms and design depth. Features that touch money or real-time data cost the most.

### Can I reduce the cost without lowering quality?

Yes. Build a true MVP, keep the backend managed at first, buy commodity capabilities instead of building them, and ship on one shared codebase for iOS and Android.

### Are these prices fixed?

No. They are planning ranges based on typical scope. A fixed price is agreed after discovery, once requirements and risks are clear.

### How long does it take to build each type of app?

Roughly 7 weeks for the MVP example, 25 for the market-ready example and 48 for the AI platform example, using the same method. See [how long it takes to build an app](/blog/how-long-to-build-an-app) for timelines in more detail.
